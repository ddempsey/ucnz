    How funding is spread. Each grant's total value is divided evenly across the days it is active, then summed by calendar year. Grants with start and end dates in NZRIS use them. Marsden grants have no dates and are spread over 3 years from award; HRC grants are spread over 5 years (≥ $3M, programmes), 3 years (≥ $500k, projects), 2 years (≥ $100k) or 1 year. These are marked assumed.

    Institutions. NZRIS records only the main grant holder. Money a grant subcontracts to partner organisations is not represented: it stays with the holder here. Crown research institutes are shown under the public research organisations they joined in 2025, and university research trusts and commercial arms are counted with their university.

    Topics. Fields of Research are ANZSRC 2020 divisions and groups. Grants before 2021 carry 2008 codes, mapped to 2020 groups with the ABS correspondence table; a 2008 field that maps to several 2020 groups is split evenly between them. "FoR share only" counts the grant's percentage in the chosen division or group. About a third of grants, mostly small community and outreach awards, have no FoR codes and appear only under themes. Indigenous studies exists only in the 2020 codes, so it starts in 2021. Themes come from a keyword search of titles and descriptions, with every match reviewed by hand; a grant can be in several themes.

    Gaps. NZRIS is missing most HRC funding after 2022. HRC announced $53.7M of project grants and $35M of programme grants in its 2023 round, but NZRIS holds no HRC award over $300k from 2023 onwards. The fall in health funding after 2021 is therefore missing data, not a funding cut. NZRIS also has no 2025 Marsden round and few 2026 awards. SSIF (institutional) funding is included and is large for the Crown research institutes.
