Where stored water comes from

ENCN342 — Hydraulics and Applied Hydrology
University of Canterbury

An aquifer gives up water when you lower its head. How much water, per metre of head drop, is the storativity — and it differs between confined and unconfined aquifers by a factor of thousands, because the water is released by two completely different physical mechanisms.

It pays to get these two straight. Almost everything else in this topic — drawdown, well yield, capture, how fast an aquifer responds — runs through $S$.

Where this fits: after Module 1 (Groundwater Systems), alongside Tutorial 3. Do this one before the capture widget — capture is about where pumped water comes from over time, and that argument only works once you know what storage actually is.
Before you look: two aquifers, same area, same 10 m of head drop — one confined, one unconfined. How much more water does the unconfined one give up?
