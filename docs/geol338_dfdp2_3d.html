DFDP-2B in Three Dimensions

GEOL338 · the hole that walked with the fabric

Drilled vertically into the Alpine Fault hanging wall, DFDP-2B began to curve at about 300 m and never stopped. It reached 893 m along the hole but only 816 m down, a quarter of a kilometre north-north-west of where it started, tracking the up-dip direction of the foliation it was cutting1.

Notes and sources
  1. Trajectory from the mean hole azimuth and deviation of the 13 televiewer logging runs in Table A1 of Massiot et al. (2018), doi.org/10.1029/2017GC007368, integrated along measured depth. Azimuths are geographic (declination 23.15°E already removed). The integration gives 816 m true vertical depth at 893 m measured depth; the report quotes 818 m. Casing shoe in basement at 264 m. The J-shaped curve has a radius of about 700 m (GNS Science Report 2015/50, Figure 65).
  2. Lower hemisphere, equal area. Foliation planes in the televiewer log average 57°→145° in the preliminary interpretation (SR 2015/50, Figures 66 and 67) and 60°→145° in Massiot et al. (2018). The pole cloud here is synthetic, drawn around that mean with a spread chosen to look like Figure 67, not the measured data. The fault great circle uses the dip you choose and the regional dip direction of 145°.
  3. Each dot is the direction the hole was heading at that depth: a vertical hole plots at the centre, and the dots walk outward as inclination grows. The star is the pole to the foliation, at azimuth 325° and plunge 30°. The hole drifted toward the foliation up-dip direction, 10° to 30° clockwise of it, which the report attributes to the clockwise rotation of the bit climbing the northern side of the hole.
  4. Cuttings stratigraphy after Toy et al. (2017) as summarised by Massiot et al. (2018): Quaternary sediments to 239 m, Alpine Schist to 280 m, protomylonite to 852 m, mylonite to 893 m.
  5. The fault was not reached. The pre-drill plan assumed a 40° dip; foliation in the hole suggests about 60°; Massiot et al. shade the plausible fault position for dips of 50° to 70°. Drilling stopped an estimated 100 to 200 m short. The two sliders span those ranges. The dip direction is fixed at 145°, the regional dip direction of the fault and the foliation.
